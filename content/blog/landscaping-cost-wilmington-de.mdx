## What makes a Wilmington quote higher or lower?

**Lot type.** City lots in Trolley Square or the Highlands often mean street parking, alley access, and hand-carried materials — more labor hours for the same square footage. Suburban Brandywine Hundred lots with driveway access price leaner.

**Mature trees.** Gorgeous, and expensive to work around. Heavy leaf loads raise cleanup prices; root zones complicate digging and planting.

**Soil and water.** If your project sits on soggy clay, honest contractors price the base prep and drainage the site needs. The cheap quote that ignores water is the expensive quote in three years.

**Season.** Spring is peak demand. Booking cleanups in late winter or patios in late summer/fall often gets you faster scheduling — and crews that aren't rushing between jobs.

## Where homeowners get the most value

1. **Fall aeration + overseeding** — the highest-impact few hundred dollars a Delaware fescue lawn can receive.
2. **Buried downspouts** — cheap insurance against foundation and basement problems.
3. **Foundation bed refresh** — for curb appeal per dollar, replacing overgrown foundation shrubs beats almost everything, especially before listing a home.
4. **Edging + fresh mulch** — the "instant maintenance" look for a few hundred dollars a season.

## How to compare quotes fairly

Any two landscaping quotes are only comparable if they specify the same scope. Look for: excavation/base depth on hardscape, plant sizes (a 3-gallon shrub is not a 7-gallon shrub), mulch depth and type, haul-away included or not, and whether the quote is fixed or an estimate. If a quote is one line and a number, ask for the breakdown — we always provide one.
